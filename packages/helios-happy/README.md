# Helios Happy

A no-lettering Living Icon personality pack for Chris/Helios. Helios is a small living sun with eyeballs: the eyes and corona carry the personality while motion carries voice state.

## Layout

- `softpass/avatar-idle.gif` — default 128px platform avatar; calm idle entry
- `matrix/{idle,listening,talking}/{calm,excited,somber,angry,thrilled}.gif` — complete 3×5 mood/state matrix

## Motion

Idle has a slow glow and corona rotation. Listening adds a pulse and blink. Talking adds bounce and mouth motion. Calm is the warm steady baseline; excited is bright and active; somber is cool and half-lidded; angry is hot red-orange with narrowed eyes; thrilled is a full flare burst with wide eyes.
